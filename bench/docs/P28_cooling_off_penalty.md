# RupeeRush: Instant Personal Loans

**Lender:** Kaveri Finance (NBFC). **Platform:** RupeeRush app by RushTech Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Personal loans of ₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000 for 3 to 12 months.

## 2. Cooling-off
Borrowers who want to exit within 3 days must repay the principal plus the full first month's interest and a 4% foreclosure charge.

## 3. Unlisted charges
After disbursal, a ₹199 "credit insights subscription" is added to every EMI. It is not in the KFS and borrowers are not asked to agree to it.

## 4. KFS
The KFS has a unique proposal number and is valid for 3 working days.

## 5. Disbursal
Loans are disbursed into the borrower's bank account.
