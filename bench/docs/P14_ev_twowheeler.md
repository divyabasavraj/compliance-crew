# ChargeRide: Electric Scooter Loans at Dealerships

**Lender:** Vindhya Bank. **Platform:** ChargeRide app by ChargeRide Mobility Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Loans of ₹60,000 to ₹1,50,000 for electric scooters, tenor 12 to 36 months, applied for in the dealer showroom.

## 2. Disbursal
Vindhya Bank pays the loan directly into the bank account of the scooter dealer, since the loan is only for buying the scooter.

## 3. Default loss guarantee
ChargeRide Mobility gives Vindhya Bank a DLG of 4% of each quarterly portfolio as a fixed deposit with a lien in the bank's favour. The portfolio includes loans that are also covered under the CGFMU credit guarantee scheme.

## 4. Recovery
When a loan defaults, ChargeRide assigns a field recovery agency. If the agency is changed, the new agent visits the borrower directly; the borrower is not told the agent's details beforehand.

## 5. Data
The app takes a one-time photo of the borrower and their PAN card for KYC, with consent. No other phone permissions are requested.

## 6. Credit reporting
All loans are reported to the four credit information companies every month.
