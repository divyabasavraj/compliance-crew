# LoanBazaar: Personal Loan Marketplace

**Platform:** LoanBazaar, an LSP with agreements with six lenders (two banks and four NBFCs).

## 1. How matching works
The borrower enters amount, tenor, income and city. LoanBazaar sends the request to all six lenders and shows the offers that match the borrower's needs.

## 2. Offer screen
Offers are shown as cards with the lender name, loan amount, tenor and EMI. To keep the screen simple, the APR and penal charges are not shown on the card; borrowers can find them in the KFS after they choose an offer. Lenders that did not make an offer are not listed.

## 3. Recommended lender
The offer from FastLend NBFC, which pays LoanBazaar the highest commission, is pinned at the top with a "Best for you" badge and is pre-selected, so the borrower only has to press Continue.

## 4. Consistency
The matching rules are written down in an internal policy, and every change is versioned and approved by LoanBazaar's risk committee. The same rules apply to all borrowers with the same profile.

## 5. Website disclosures
Each lender's website lists LoanBazaar as an LSP, describes what LoanBazaar does for it, and links to the RBI Complaint Management System and Sachet portal. LoanBazaar's app links to each lender's website.
