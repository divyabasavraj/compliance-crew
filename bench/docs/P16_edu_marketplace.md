# SkillLoan: Marketplace for Course Loans

**Platform:** SkillLoan by Upskill Credit Pvt Ltd (LSP), partnered with five lenders (two banks and three NBFCs).

## 1. Product
Learners choose a course and see loan offers from partner lenders for the course fee, ₹20,000 to ₹3,00,000.

## 2. Matching
Which lenders are shown depends on a rule set the sales team adjusts weekly. Borrowers with the same profile may see different lenders on different days, and the rules are not written down anywhere.

## 3. Offer display
Each offer shows the lender's name, loan amount, tenor, APR, monthly instalment and penal charges. Offers are ranked by APR, lowest first, and the app states this ranking rule at the top of the list.

## 4. Disbursal
The course fee is paid by the lender directly into the training institute's bank account.

## 5. Data storage
Upskill Credit keeps borrowers' bank statements, salary slips and credit bureau reports on its own servers for three years to improve its matching models.
