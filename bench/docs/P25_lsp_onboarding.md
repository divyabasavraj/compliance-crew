# Partner Onboarding Note: Ganga Finance and FastFunds

**Regulated entity:** Ganga Finance (NBFC). **LSP:** FastFunds Digital Pvt Ltd.

## 1. Arrangement
FastFunds will source borrowers, run the app and handle collections for Ganga Finance. The two sides agreed the terms on a call and will start lending next week; no written contract has been signed.

## 2. Due diligence
Ganga Finance did not review FastFunds' technology, data privacy policies or past conduct before agreeing, because FastFunds is a well-known brand.

## 3. Collections
FastFunds will also act as the recovery agent for overdue loans. Ganga Finance has given FastFunds no guidance or training on fair recovery practices.

## 4. Disbursal and repayment
Loans are disbursed to borrowers' bank accounts and repaid directly to Ganga Finance.

## 5. Data
FastFunds keeps only the borrower's name, phone and address needed for collections.
