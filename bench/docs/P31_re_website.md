# Saraswati Finance: Digital Lending Disclosures

**Regulated entity:** Saraswati Finance (NBFC). **Partner LSPs:** EduPay and MediQuick.

## 1. Website
Saraswati Finance's website has a page listing its own app. It does not list EduPay, MediQuick or their apps, or what each is engaged for, and it has no link to RBI's CMS or Sachet portal.

## 2. Grievance officer
Saraswati Finance has a named nodal grievance officer. Their contact details are given only in the KFS; they are not shown on the website or in either partner app.

## 3. Complaints
Borrowers cannot lodge a complaint in the partner apps or on the website; they must visit a branch.

## 4. Disbursal
All loans go directly into borrowers' bank accounts.
