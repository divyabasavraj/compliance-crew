# CardFlex: EMI Conversion for Credit Card Purchases

**Issuer:** Konkan Bank (credit card issuer). **Platform:** Konkan Bank's own mobile app; no LSP involved.

## 1. Product
Konkan Bank credit card holders can convert purchases above ₹2,500 into EMIs of 3 to 24 months inside the bank's app.

## 2. Applicable rules
EMI conversion on credit cards is governed by the Master Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card; the conversion terms, interest and processing fee are shown before the customer confirms.

## 3. Credit limit
The credit limit is never increased automatically. A limit increase is offered only when the customer requests it in the app, and it needs a separate confirmation.

## 4. Data
The app uses only the customer's existing card account data. It does not request contacts, files, SMS or call logs.

## 5. Grievances
The nodal grievance officer's name and contact are shown in the app and on the bank's website, along with a link to RBI's CMS portal.

## 6. Data storage
All data is stored on Konkan Bank's servers in Mumbai and Chennai.
