# FaceFin: One-Look Personal Loans

**Lender:** Nilgiri Finserv (NBFC). **Platform:** FaceFin app, built and run by Nilgiri Finserv itself.

## 1. Product
Unsecured personal loans of ₹20,000 to ₹3,00,000 for 6 to 36 months.

## 2. Face login
To make login fast and secure, FaceFin stores each customer's face template and fingerprint data on its servers and uses them for every login and loan approval.

## 3. Data hosting
Our analytics and core lending systems run on a cloud provider's Singapore data centre, chosen for lower cost. A copy of the data is synced to India every quarter.

## 4. Consent controls
In the Privacy Centre, borrowers can switch off consent for specific data uses, block sharing with third parties, choose how long data is kept, withdraw consent, and request deletion of their data.

## 5. Privacy policy
A full privacy policy is published on the website and in the app, listing the third parties that collect data through the app and the data storage, retention and destruction rules.

## 6. Creditworthiness
Before every loan we collect and keep on record the borrower's age, occupation, employer and monthly income from salary slips.
