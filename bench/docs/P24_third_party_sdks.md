# SwiftLoan: App Analytics and Partners

**Lender:** Nilgiri Bank. **Platform:** SwiftLoan app by SwiftStack Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Personal loans of ₹20,000 to ₹3,00,000 through the SwiftLoan app.

## 2. Third-party SDKs
The app includes three third-party SDKs (an analytics SDK, an advertising SDK and a device-fingerprinting SDK) that collect device identifiers and in-app behaviour. The privacy policy on the website does not name these third parties.

## 3. Device access
For fraud checks, the app scans the list of installed apps and reads files in the download folder.

## 4. Data location
All data, including SDK data, is stored on servers in Mumbai.

## 5. Grievances
Nodal grievance officers of Nilgiri Bank and SwiftStack are named on both websites, in the app and in the KFS.
