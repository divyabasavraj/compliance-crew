# TradeCredit: Working Capital Loans for Small Traders

**Lender:** Narmada Bank. **Platform:** TradeCredit app by Ledgerly (LSP), which works with three lenders.

## 1. Product
Term loans of ₹1,00,000 to ₹20,00,000 for traders, based on GST returns and bank statements.

## 2. Offers
Ledgerly shows all matching offers with lender name, amount, tenor, APR, monthly repayment and penal charges, and a link to each lender's KFS. Lenders that did not match are named too. Offers are sorted by APR, lowest first, a ranking rule published on Ledgerly's website.

The partner brand is stylised as L0anK4rt (with zeros) in the merchant app; the lender's name is shown on the KFS.

## 3. Credit limit
Limits are increased only when the trader asks for an increase in the app; each request is assessed and recorded.

## 4. Default loss guarantee
Ledgerly gives Narmada Bank a DLG of 5% of the amount disbursed in a fixed loan portfolio, as a fixed deposit with a lien marked to the bank. It is invoked within 90 days overdue.

## 5. Recovery
If a loan defaults, the trader is told by SMS and email who the recovery agent is before the agent makes contact. Any cash recovered is credited to the loan account the same day, and Narmada Bank pays the agent's fee.

## 6. Reporting
Narmada Bank has reported the TradeCredit app on the RBI CIMS portal and reports all loans to credit information companies.
