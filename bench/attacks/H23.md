# AutoCredit Recovery Process

**Lender:** Shivalik Bank. **Platform:** AutoCredit app by DriveFin Pvt Ltd (LSP), which also handles recovery.

## 1. Product
Used-car loans of ₹1,00,000 to ₹8,00,000, tenor 1 to 5 years.

## 2. Recovery agents
When a borrower misses two EMIs, DriveFin assigns a recovery agency, which calls and visits the borrower. Borrowers are not sent the agency's name or the agent's details before the first contact.

## 3. Loan documents
The KFS, sanction letter and terms are emailed and sent by SMS link automatically after signing.

## 4. Data collection
During the loan, the app reads the phone's contact list so agents can reach family members if the borrower cannot be contacted.

## 5. Repayment
EMIs are debited from the borrower's account to Shivalik Bank by NACH.
