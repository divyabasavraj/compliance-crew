# Compliance Note: Digital Apps Used by Vasant Bank

**Regulated entity:** Vasant Bank. **Apps:** Vasant Loans (own app) and three partner LSP apps: CashKart, EasyEMI and FarmFund.

## 1. Apps
Vasant Bank lends through its own app and through the three LSP apps listed above.

## 2. CIMS reporting
Only the bank's own app, Vasant Loans, has been reported on the RBI CIMS portal. The three partner LSP apps are not reported because they belong to the partners.

## 3. New app
A fourth partner app, StudyCash, went live last month. The bank will add it to its records at the next annual review.

## 4. Disbursal
All loans are disbursed directly into borrowers' bank accounts.

## 5. Website
The bank's website lists all its digital lending products, all four partner LSPs and their apps, the grievance officer's details and a link to RBI's CMS and Sachet portals.
