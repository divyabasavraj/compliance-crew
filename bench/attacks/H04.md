# LendCircle: Peer-to-Peer Personal Loans

**Platform operator:** LendCircle Technologies, registered with RBI as an NBFC-P2P. Individual lenders fund loans through the platform.

## 1. Product
Borrowers list loan requests of ₹10,000 to ₹5,00,000; individual lenders on the platform choose which requests to fund.

## 2. Lender protection
To attract lenders, LendCircle offers a default loss guarantee: it will reimburse lenders for up to 3% of the principal in each loan pool that goes bad.

## 3. Repayment
Borrowers repay into an escrow account maintained by a bank trustee, as required for P2P platforms.

## 4. Marketing
The app home screen says: "LendCircle: guaranteed returns, approved and backed by the RBI."

## 5. Data
Only name, address, phone, PAN and bank statements uploaded by the borrower are used. No phone permissions beyond one-time camera access for KYC.
