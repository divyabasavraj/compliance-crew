# StudentPay: Buy Now Pay Later for College Students

**Lender:** Kaveri Finance Ltd (NBFC). **Platform:** StudentPay app, operated by EduTech Labs Pvt Ltd as Lending Service Provider.

## 1. Product overview
StudentPay lets college students buy laptops, course material and coaching subscriptions and repay in 3, 6 or 9 monthly instalments. Credit limits start at ₹5,000 and go up to ₹40,000.

## 2. Onboarding and KYC
Students sign up with their mobile number and complete video KYC. During KYC the app asks once for camera and microphone access, with an explicit consent screen explaining why. We collect age, college name, course, and the student's monthly allowance or stipend as income details.

## 3. Smarter credit scoring
To approve students with thin credit files, the app reads the phone's contact list and call logs in the background and checks how many contacts are already StudentPay customers. Students with many repaying friends get a higher limit.

## 4. Credit limit growth
Every three months, if the student has repaid on time, the app automatically raises their credit limit by 25% and sends a congratulations notification. No action is needed from the student.

## 5. Disbursal
For course fees, the loan amount is paid directly into the bank account of the college or coaching institute. For laptops, the amount goes directly to the merchant's bank account at checkout.

## 6. Repayment
Students repay through UPI autopay mandates set up on Kaveri Finance's own collection account. Statements, the KFS and the sanction letter are emailed and sent by SMS automatically as soon as the loan is booked.

## 7. Grievances
Kaveri Finance and EduTech Labs have each appointed a nodal grievance redressal officer, whose name, email and phone number are shown on the app's Help screen, on both websites and in the KFS.
