# CarKey: Used-Car Loans Through Dealers

**Lender:** Pench Bank. **Platform:** CarKey app by CarKey Mobility Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Loans of ₹1,50,000 to ₹10,00,000 for certified used cars, tenor 1 to 6 years.

Dealers are told not to promise approval; the app shows the lender's decision only after the credit check.

## 2. Disbursal
The loan is paid directly into the dealer's bank account, because the loan is only for buying that car.

## 3. Default loss guarantee
CarKey Mobility, a company under the Companies Act, 2013, provides a DLG of 5% of each portfolio as cash deposited with Pench Bank, invoked within 90 days overdue, under a Board-approved policy. The DLG runs for 7 years, longer than any loan in the portfolio. CarKey publishes its DLG portfolios and amounts on its website every month within seven working days.

## 4. Data
One-time camera and location access during onboarding for KYC and address checks, with consent. Data is stored on servers in India.

## 5. Credit reporting and grievances
All loans are reported to CICs. Nodal grievance officers of both companies are named on both websites, in the app and in the KFS.
