# PayDay Bridge: Salary Advance for Factory Workers

**Lender:** Narmada Finance (NBFC). **Platform:** PayDay Bridge app by Bridgework Labs Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Workers at partner factories can borrow up to 50% of their monthly salary for up to 30 days. The loan is repaid in one instalment on salary day.

## 2. Disbursal
The loan amount is credited to the worker's own salary bank account within 10 minutes. Bridgework Labs deducts its ₹150 service fee from the amount before it is credited, so the worker receives the loan minus the fee.

## 3. Data collection
To check repayment capacity, the app reads the worker's SMS inbox for salary credit messages and bank alerts every week.

## 4. KFS
The KFS is shown in the app in English only. Most users are Hindi- and Odia-speaking workers; there is no translation and no step to confirm the worker has understood it.

## 5. Cooling-off
Workers can cancel within 3 days by repaying the principal and proportionate interest, with no penalty.

## 6. Grievances
Narmada Finance and Bridgework Labs each have a named nodal grievance officer, listed on both websites, in the app and in the KFS.
