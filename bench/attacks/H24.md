# KYCFast: Video KYC and Biometric Onboarding

**Lender:** Narmada Bank. **Platform:** KYCFast app by IDFlow Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Personal loans of ₹25,000 to ₹2,00,000, with fully digital onboarding.

## 2. Aadhaar eKYC
Aadhaar-based eKYC is done through the bank's licensed KYC user agency under the Aadhaar Act; the bank receives only the eKYC response.

## 3. Fingerprint archive
IDFlow additionally saves a copy of each borrower's fingerprint scan on its own servers, to speed up repeat loans.

## 4. Statements
IDFlow keeps full copies of borrowers' bank statements and salary slips on its servers after the loan is approved.

## 5. Retention
Neither Narmada Bank nor IDFlow has a data retention or deletion policy; data is kept indefinitely and nothing is published about storage.

## 6. Data location
All data is stored on servers in Hyderabad.
