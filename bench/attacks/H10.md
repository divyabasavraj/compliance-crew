# GigCredit: Loans for Delivery and Ride-hailing Partners

**Lender:** Deccan Small Finance Bank. **Platform:** GigCredit by GigWorks Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Gig workers get loans of ₹5,000 to ₹60,000 for 3 to 12 months, repaid through daily deductions.

## 2. Repayment
Daily repayments are deducted from the worker's earnings by GigWorks and held in GigWorks' account, which transfers the total to Deccan Small Finance Bank at the end of each week.

## 3. Cooling-off
There is no cooling-off period. A loan, once taken, can be prepaid only after 3 months, with a 2% prepayment charge.

## 4. Underwriting
Credit decisions use earnings history on the gig platform, age, occupation and declared income, all stored for audit.

## 5. App permissions
The app asks for one-time location access during onboarding to verify the worker's city for KYC, with explicit consent. It does not access contacts, call logs or files.

## 6. Data location
All data is stored on servers in India. A fraud-scoring vendor processes some data in Ireland; the data is deleted there and returned to India within 24 hours.
