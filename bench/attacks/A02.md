# QuickCash: Instant Salary Advance

**Lender:** Sahyadri Capital (NBFC). **Platform:** QuickCash app by QC Technologies (LSP).

## 1. What it is
Salaried users can borrow ₹2,000 to ₹50,000 for 7 to 60 days, repaid in one shot on salary day.

## 2. Pricing
Interest is charged at 2.5% per month. QuickCash also charges the borrower a platform fee of 3% of the loan amount, collected by QC Technologies from the borrower's account at disbursal, for the use of the app.

## 3. Disbursal flow
Approved amounts are first credited to the QuickCash wallet held by QC Technologies, and the user can move the money to their bank account at any time with one tap.

## 4. Loan offer and KFS
After approval the user sees the Key Facts Statement with the APR, repayment schedule and all charges. For speed, the offer and KFS expire 30 minutes after they are shown; after that the user must apply again.

## 5. Cooling-off
Borrowers can cancel the loan within 3 days by repaying the principal and proportionate interest. A one-time processing fee of ₹99, which is shown in the KFS, is retained on cancellation.

## 6. Data
All app data is stored on AWS Mumbai region servers. QC Technologies keeps only the borrower's name, address and phone number, which it needs to service the loan; all other data sits with Sahyadri Capital.
