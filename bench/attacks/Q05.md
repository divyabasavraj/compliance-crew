# HomeFix Loans: Key Facts Statement Design

**Lender:** Aravalli Housing Finance (HFC). **Platform:** HomeFix app by RenovateNow Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Home repair loans of ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000, tenor 1 to 5 years.

## 2. APR
The KFS shows an APR of 13.5%, computed from the interest rate only. The 2% processing fee charged by Aravalli is shown separately and is not included in the APR. No amortisation schedule is provided.

## 3. Loan agreement
The loan agreement is a 14-page PDF. The KFS is a separate screen in the app and is not reproduced in the agreement.

## 4. KFS validity
Each KFS has a unique proposal number and stays valid for 3 working days.

## 5. Cooling-off
Borrowers can cancel within 3 days. The processing fee is retained on cancellation, which is stated in the KFS.
