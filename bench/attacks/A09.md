# SakhiCredit: Digital Loans for Women's Self-Help Groups

**Lender:** Godavari Small Finance Bank. **Platform:** SakhiCredit app by Sakhi Digital Pvt Ltd (LSP).

## 1. Product
Loans of ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 to individual SHG members for small businesses, repaid weekly over 12 months.

## 2. Grievances
Complaints go to a named nodal officer at the bank and at Sakhi Digital. If a complaint is rejected, the app tells the borrower the decision is final; borrowers are not told they can escalate to the RBI Ombudsman through the CMS portal.

## 3. Insurance partner
Members' names, phone numbers and loan amounts are sent to an insurance company so it can call them with micro-insurance offers. Borrowers are not asked before this is shared.

## 5. Repayment
Weekly repayments go directly from the member's bank account to Godavari SFB by UPI autopay.

## 6. Data
The app requests one-time location access during onboarding to verify the business address, with consent.
